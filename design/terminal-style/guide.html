04 / BRAND IDENTITY

Keep it simply maro.

A lowercase wordmark with quiet confidence. The name remains Maro; the visual character shifts toward a personal music workstation.

maroFoam / dark
JetBrains Mono / 600
maroAbyss / light
Single-color wordmark

Visual identity

  • Four lowercase letters. One color.
  • Standalone clear space: one lowercase m on each side.
  • In-app wordmark: current 24 pt size, frame and position.
  • ASCII details belong in artwork or brand materials.
  • Keep native control symbols and real album art.

Voice & restraint

  • Calm, direct and useful.
  • “Search your library.” “Add to playlist.” “Retry.”
  • Keep existing application copy during the visual migration.
  • Short labels can be uppercase; music titles stay natural.
  • Skip fake telemetry, command syntax, cursor blink and glow.
05 / BACKGROUND & CUSTOMIZATION

Atmosphere, on your terms.

Solid color first. Later, personal background images and theme colors can change the mood while geometry stays locked.

OPTIONAL / OCEAN MIST

Depth without distraction.

Original vector landscape. A 92–100% Ocean veil protects text contrast; blur adds atmosphere.

Theme fields, later

  • Semantic colors and constrained font choices.
  • Background image, crop, tint and blur.
  • Panel opacity inside a readable range.
  • Preview, reset, import and export.
  • Settings accessed through the existing account menu.

Readability remains stable

  • Controls and text-heavy surfaces remain opaque in v1.
  • Reduced Transparency returns to opaque surfaces.
  • Reduced Motion removes decorative transitions.
  • Backgrounds never alter panel or button positions.
