## Result

1. Collapsing the library keeps a 72-point rail with expansion, creation, Favorites and playlist artwork. Covers have 60-point button targets, tooltips, accessible names and selected state. Favorites and playlist navigation were exercised in the installed app. The expanded collapse control remains accessible and becomes visible on keyboard focus.
2. Bottom transport uses symmetric side allocations across the full app width. Native rendered Play-circle measurements at 760, 900, 1024, 1200 and 1440 points, in both sidebar states, all measured **0.0 points** of horizontal center offset.
3. Recommendation cards expose a secondary 28-point plus control on hover or keyboard focus, separated from the 44-point artwork play control. The themed popup searches owned playlists, supports multiple selections, identifies known saved membership, and retains Favorites. A batch reserves the library across destinations, reports individual outcomes, skips confirmed membership, and blocks further writes after an ambiguous result. Failed destinations can be retried without repeating successful adds.
4. Search rows clip real artwork after the fixed 52-point frame and separate title/creator from duration and actions. Real JPEG renders were inspected at narrow and wide widths.
5. Search requests an initial 25 tracks and loads subsequent 25-track pages when the search scroll footer becomes visible. The pinned extractor's lazy YouTube continuation iterator supplies pages; bounded cursors can reconstruct after worker restart. Query-generation, cancellation, concurrent-request, duplicate-item and no-progress safeguards are covered by focused tests. Loading, error, retry and end states remain. A live installed search loaded another page after reaching the bottom.
6. Search actions use a literal vertical three-dot glyph with the menu indicator hidden. Native macOS rendering verified the orientation and absence of the chevron; Play, Favorites and Add to playlist actions remain.
