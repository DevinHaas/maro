# Research Questions

1. What is the repository's current baseline, including tracked and untracked files, application source, configuration, dependency manifests, build and run entry points, tests, and the boundary between repository content and HumanLayer task metadata?
2. How does SketchyBar's current runtime architecture work across item configuration, executable scripts, CLI-driven updates, built-in and custom events, click handlers, long-running helper processes, and `mach_helper`, including process lifecycle and timeout constraints?
3. What presentation, interaction, and design-system primitives does SketchyBar currently expose for popups, images and background images, labels, pointer events, keyboard or text entry, and theming; and what patterns do its official examples establish for colors (including hex values), typography, spacing, borders, shadows, and state changes?
4. What interfaces do the current official YouTube APIs provide for search, result filtering, pagination, video metadata, and thumbnails, and how do authentication, quotas, errors, and result availability behave?
5. How do YouTube's current officially supported playback interfaces behave for loading and navigating media, playback state, metadata, errors, autoplay, and client identification, and what platform and policy constraints govern player visibility and dimensions, background or audio-only playback, advertisements, downloading, and undocumented access?
6. How does FUTO's Grayjay currently perform source discovery, search, metadata and thumbnail retrieval, playback, queue navigation, and favorites or subscription persistence, and which parts of that architecture are shared with or specific to its desktop and macOS support?
