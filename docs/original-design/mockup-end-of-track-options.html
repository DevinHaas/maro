What happens when a video ends?

There is no queue or autoplay. The loaded video can remain available to replay, or playback can clear and return the item to its empty state.

Option A · Recommended

Keep the video loaded for replay

The bar shows an Ended state. The popup retains the thumbnail and metadata, and its transport action becomes Replay.

Option B

Clear playback and return to Search

The finished video is unloaded. The bar becomes a Search entry point again; replay requires finding it in search or favorites.
